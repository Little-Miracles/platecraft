# PlateCraft

A calorie tracker that counts your own home-cooked food exactly — from the real ingredients and the weight of the finished dish.

## The Unique Kernel
Build your kitchen once, then know your calories exactly.

**Exact, not approximate.** You build a dish from the actual products and grams you used, enter the weight of the finished dish, and get the true calories per 100 g of *your* soup or *your* cupcake. No AI guessing: a photo of a cocktail that only "sees" the lemon, or the same cup of coffee counted once as 46 kcal and once as 98 kcal. Other apps offer "borscht from a database"; PlateCraft counts *your* borscht.

## Who It's For
Someone who watches calories and cooks for themselves (or knows how their food is made). Today they pick a generic dish from a database or let AI estimate from a photo or voice — "plus or minus a tram stop." The first such person is the learner herself.

## The Core Loop
Open the app → pick a meal (breakfast, lunch, dinner, snack, water) → pick a category (drinks, baked goods, soups, main dishes) → either enter a ready-made item (name + calories + amount eaten) or build a homemade one from ingredients → save → the home banner shows calories eaten against the daily target.

## Inspiration & Identity
Colors from Smart Body Life: emerald, gold, purple. Logo: a plate split into sectors (ingredients) with a spoon and a knife. Feel: premium, calm, precise.

## Why This Matters to the Learner
"I'm a person who likes everything to be scrupulous, as precise as possible. I couldn't find this kind of counter anywhere — I'm making it for myself, and I think many people who track calories miss it too." Also: prize money for a new laptop; the dish builder later moves into her app Smart Body Life.

## What "Working" Looks Like
Open PlateCraft → lunch → soup → homemade → add ingredients with grams (3 L water, shrimp, potatoes…) → enter the finished soup's weight and how much was eaten → see **kcal per 100 g and per portion** → save → the home banner's eaten calories **increase**.
The demo video's story is decided later, once the learner sees the working app.

## The POC Boundary
- Start screen with the logo → home screen: banner (daily target / eaten / water) + two buttons (Calculator, food builder).
- Calculator: sex, age, height, weight, activity → daily calories and water; avatar chosen from an icon set.
- Five meals (breakfast, lunch, dinner, snack, water) → four categories (drinks, baked goods, soups, main dishes).
- Ready-made path: name + kcal per 100 g + amount eaten.
- Homemade path: ingredients with grams + finished weight → kcal per 100 g and per portion. One builder serves every category.
- **My Kitchen library** (learner: "this is the most important"): a homemade dish is saved once — cook borscht once, next time just weigh the plate. Each dish gets a volumetric 3D icon from a built-in set.
- Small built-in product table (~40 items) + the user can add a product with kcal from its package.
- Water: "+ glass" button.

## Later
- Own photos for dishes and avatar (heavy in a browser prototype; natural next step inside Smart Body Life).
- History by day, macros (protein / fat / carbs), moving the builder into Smart Body Life.

## Explicitly Cut
- AI, photo and voice recognition — the whole point is exact counting, not estimates.
- Login and cloud sync — not needed to prove the kernel.
